  • The frame spans the full width. Pinch zooms the view, never the page.
  • One primary action, in thumb reach, above the safe area.
  • The tray sits above the system keyboard: text goes on Send, and the key row shows only keys this Screen accepts.
  • The Notification opens here directly, and the reason stays visible without covering the page.
  • The sheet replaces the old 126 dp rail. Cards are live thumbnails, and only visible ones stream.
  • Taking over here moves the generation forward, and the other Device's input is refused.
  • The stale frame is greyed and input is off until a fresh frame arrives. Nothing is replayed.
  • Watching only, so input is off. The Agent's target is marked on the frame.

Signature: the frame edge and its tag show who drives the Screen. Accent: the Agent acts. Warning: it asks for you. Success: you're in control. Grey: someone else, or stale. The same component serves Computers. Their cards drop the address bar and show platform keys (Back, Home) instead of Tab and Esc.
